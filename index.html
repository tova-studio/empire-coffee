About us

About Empire Coffee

Empire Coffee is a specialty, quality driven mobile coffee vendor operating from the wider Christchurch region. We’re a small, focused Christchurch business with a clear idea of what good coffee should be, and we work hard to serve it every day.

“Damn good coffee.” Three words, and we take every one of them seriously.
